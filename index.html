Putnam County Election Results, 2024 & 2026 MAP

Every race on the Putnam County ballot in both elections, precinct by precinct — 48 races in 2026 and 22 in 2024, across 31 precincts. Switch years with the toggle; the offices change with it, because Indiana elects its statewide row in midterms and its federal row in presidential years. Shade the map by head-to-head margin, any candidate's share of the vote, or ballots cast. Four races carry a precinct-level baseline for the shift since 2022 or 2024, including the top of the ticket — President in 2024 against Secretary of State in 2026. 2026 counts are preliminary.

Indiana County Shift, 2022 and 2024 to 2026 MAP

All 92 Indiana counties, shaded by how far each moved toward or away from the Democrat. Six races: Secretary of State, Comptroller and Treasurer against 2022; US Representative and State Representative against either 2022 or 2024; and the top of the ticket, which pairs each year's head of ballot — US Senate in 2022, President in 2024, Secretary of State in 2026. Every move is given two ways: what the Democrat gained as a share of all votes cast, and how far the gap between the two parties shifted. Counting is still underway, so unreported counties are hatched out and headline figures use only the counties that have finished.
